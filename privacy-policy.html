Roadster Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Roadster Rush ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used. The app is published by NG-Innovate.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the app.

Game data stored on your device

Your best score, bolts (in-game currency), upgrades, paint jobs, awards, lifetime game statistics and sound settings are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location (such as country or city),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic information about crashes and performance of the ads SDK.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Depending on your choices it may be used to show personalized ads. Learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether Google may use your data for personalized ads. You can change this at any time from Privacy settings on the game's main menu.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ads will then no longer be personalized using it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.

Optional rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded ad (to continue a run or double your bolts) is always optional. No additional personal information is collected when you choose to watch one.

App updates

The app uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This check is handled by Google Play on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by Google AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
